## 7–9 mapping used by Teacher HQ

- **Outcomes for Science, Technology and Society (STS) and Knowledge** → internal **Skills & Procedures**, flagged **Summative priority**.
- **Skill Outcomes** → internal **Knowledge**.
- **Attitude Outcomes** → internal **Understanding**.
- The UI labels Grade 7–9 Science branches as **Unit → Outcome Category → General Outcome / Skill Area** instead of pretending they are Guiding Questions / Learning Outcomes.
- Illustrative parenthetical examples beginning with “e.g.” / “for example” were excluded from the stored Grade 7–9 outcome text because the source states examples are not required program outcomes.
